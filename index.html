Process

From the field to your dock, in four controlled steps.

Each step produces a document. Together they are the approval packet your quality team receives with every shipment.

01

Source

We work with established processors in each product's leading growing region. For our current lines, that means the Fayoum and Beni Suef herb belt and the seed-growing regions of Upper Egypt. We work in the local language and audit every processing line in person before the first order.

Output: supplier audit & specification
02

Test

Before shipment, a sealed sample is drawn from the lot and sent to an accredited US laboratory for microbiology, pesticide residues, heavy metals, moisture and foreign matter. The results go to you. If a lot fails, it does not ship.

Output: certificate of analysis
03

Document

Stoneferry is the FSVP importer of record. We file FDA prior notice, hold the verification records, and assemble the packet: spec sheet, COA, prior notice confirmation, FSVP record and certificate of insurance.

Output: approval packet
04

Deliver

Sea freight, customs clearance and FDA entry are handled by us. You receive a delivered price per pound in US dollars, a delivery window, and product at your dock.

Output: delivered goods & packing list
