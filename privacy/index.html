Privacy

What this site collects

Short version: what you choose to send us, and standard usage analytics. We do not sell data and we have no mailing list.

What you send us

When you email us or use the quote form, we receive what you write: your name, company, email address and message. We use it only to answer you. The quote form is delivered to our inbox by Formspree, a form-handling service, which processes the submission on our behalf.

Site analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: which pages are opened, what is clicked, how far a page is scrolled, how long a visit lasts, and the visitor's device type and approximate location. Clarity can replay a visit as a recording of the page. Text typed into the quote form and the cost calculator is masked and is not recorded.

Clarity uses cookies. Microsoft processes this data and describes its own use of it in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics is not loaded.

Links we send you

A link in an email from us may carry a short code, so we can tell that the link was opened. The code is not your name or email address.

Questions

Write to omar@stoneferrytrading.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, or delete it.
