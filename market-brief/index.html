Method and sources

Source.

Landed cost is the customs value of the goods, plus freight and insurance to the first US port (together, the Census CIF value), plus the calculated duty. It does not include customs brokerage, inland freight, laboratory testing or treatment.

Duty paid is the Census calculated duty, an estimate of the duty collected at entry, divided by the customs value. It is an average across all shipments, not the published tariff rate. Confirm the rate for your shipment with a licensed customs broker.

Comparisons. Origins with under 2% of the weight are grouped into Other origins. Shares may not add to exactly 100% because of rounding. In the full table, per-unit figures are not shown where an origin shipped under one metric ton in the period.
